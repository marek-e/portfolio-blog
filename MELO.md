# Melo and Orbi

This document describes the character and visual identity board in `public/melo/melo.png`. The image introduces Melo, an explorer and guardian, and Orbi, a small AI companion. It combines a main illustration with character views, expressions, poses, color palettes, and examples of how the pair appear in a website or app.

## Personality and purpose

The board introduces Melo with the words "Curious explorer. Guardian at heart. Helper in the agentic coding era."

Melo represents an interest in technology, security, and building products that help people. The accompanying text describes someone who keeps learning, ships work, and helps humans do more. Four activities organize this identity:

- Explore: ask questions and discover new things.
- Build: turn ideas into products and tools that solve real problems.
- Protect: put security, privacy, and trust first, and do the right thing.
- Help: support humans and AI agents as they work together.

The main illustration repeats these ideas in the motto "Explore. Build. Protect. Help."

## Main illustration and overall style

The upper-left scene shows Melo standing on a rocky path in a mountain valley, holding an unfolded paper map. Wildflowers, grass, butterflies, distant peaks, and warm clouds surround the character. The sky shifts from peach and gold near the horizon to lavender and blue above. Orbi floats beside Melo and leaves a curling trail of golden light close to the ground.

The drawing has a soft, painted finish, with rounded forms, detailed fur, warm highlights, and gentle shadows. Melo's large head, small body, and expressive eyes give the character a chibi appearance. The outdoor adventure setting sits alongside familiar developer objects such as laptops, code symbols, and interface panels elsewhere on the board.

The board itself uses cream panels with rounded corners on a dark navy-to-purple background. Most headings are purple, body text is dark navy, and small orange or golden sparkles recur throughout the artwork. The large MELO wordmark is dark navy with orange sparkle symbols beside it.

## Melo's appearance

Melo is a small, fluffy fantasy creature with fox-like proportions and feather-like frills around the ears and sides of the head. The board does not name a species.

- A large, rounded head sits above a short, compact body.
- The face and forehead are pale peach and cream, with fluffy cheeks, a small dark nose, a smiling mouth, and freckles beneath the eyes.
- Large, glossy dark eyes have purple tones and bright reflections.
- Tall, pointed ears have dark purple interiors and warm orange edges. Layered pink, mauve, and violet frills fan outward around them.
- A pale tuft of fur rises from the top of the head.
- A glowing golden mark sits in the center of the forehead. It resembles a small sun or diamond surrounded by short rays and identifies Melo as a guardian.
- Melo wears a dark navy hoodie with purple shading, gold details, and a shield emblem on the upper sleeve. Dark trousers and small exposed paws complete the outfit.
- A brown explorer backpack has visible straps, pockets, buckles, and rolled gear attached to its side.
- A thick purple tail curves behind the body and ends in a pale cream tip.

The character design panel shows front, three-quarter, side, and back views. These make the shape of the ears, backpack, hood, and tail visible from different angles. A larger annotated view calls attention to the curious eyes, guardian mark, ear frills, fluffy cheeks, big ears, cozy hoodie, explorer backpack, and fluffy tail. The big ears are associated with being a good listener.

## Orbi, the AI companion

Orbi is a tiny floating AI sidekick that analyzes, remembers, and assists Melo and Marek. The board describes its role as helping them go further, faster, and smarter.

Orbi has a round body with a glossy black face inside a pale metallic shell. Golden eyes and a small curved mouth create a simple, friendly expression. Small wing-like fins sit on either side, with warm metallic details around the shell. A golden glow and elliptical light rings beneath Orbi suggest hovering.

## Melo in action

A four-step sequence translates the characters into a product-building workflow:

1. Understand the problem. Melo examines something through a magnifying glass beside a question bubble.
2. Plan with AI and context. Melo uses a laptop while Orbi floats beside blue information panels.
3. Build and iterate together. Melo and Orbi continue working at the laptop.
4. Test, secure, and ship. Melo holds a shield while Orbi glows nearby.

Five colored labels beneath the sequence name the values behind this work: "Curious by nature," "Security-first," "Reliable," "Open Source," and "Human-centered."
