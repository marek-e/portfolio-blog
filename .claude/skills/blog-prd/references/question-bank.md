# Question bank — Blog PRD grill

Use these as branches, not as a script to dump. One at a time. Always attach **your recommendation**.

## Thesis

**Q:** What is the one contestable sentence? If you have two claims glued with “and”, which is load-bearing?

Recommendations pattern: offer 2–4 candidate theses (mechanism vs prescription vs camouflage vs threat-model metaphor). Force a single pick.

Smell: topic labels (“X security”, “lessons from Y”).

## Intent

**Q:** Finish: “This post IS …” and “This post is NOT …”

Push hard on NOT: summary of a source article, generic explainer, incomplete scare post, etc.

## Dual-angle trap

**Q:** You want both A and B. What single thesis *requires* both as evidence (not as two topics)?

If they can’t name one, make them pick a lead and demote the other to a section.

## Evidence / finale

**Q:** What counts as the end of the proof? Harmless local PoC, loopback shell, mention-only swap, scanner-clean install, …?

**Q:** Lab rules: own machine only? no non-loopback C2? record each rung?

Recommend an earned ladder with a kill criterion: failed rungs become scars or get cut.

## Scope

**Q:** Skills-only, or agents too? What is mention-only?

Recommend widening only when the finale requires it.

## Audience

**Q:** Who is this for, and who are you willing to lose?

## Takeaways ranked

**Q:** Rank what the reader leaves with. Does #1 follow from the thesis?

Smell: “be careful” as #1 when the thesis says structural controls matter — reorder.

## Human vs structural controls

**Q:** If you want readers to *read* the file *and* set a deny-list, how do you rank them so the ending doesn’t contradict itself?

Recommend: both, ranked; name what each controls.

## Objection

**Q:** What is the strongest skeptic counter? Give it its best form, then bound or refute.

## Payoff

**Q:** What concrete action or snippet does the reader leave with?

Smell: payoff that is only diligence when thesis promised a floor control.

## Meta

**Q:** Languages? Related posts? Working title? Public PoC repo?

## Lock

**Q:** Here is the locked brief. Any field to reopen, or lock it?
